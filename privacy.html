Jarvis Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Jarvis is a private personal assistant used by a single person, its owner. It only accesses Google accounts that belong to its owner.

Data Jarvis accesses

With the owner's permission, Jarvis uses these Google services:

  • Google Calendar: to read, create, change and delete events.
  • Gmail: to read messages, and to create drafts and send email when the owner asks.
  • Google Contacts: to look up email addresses.

How data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's requests and to send the owner briefings and reminders. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

Services that process data

To do its job, Jarvis passes content to these services:

  • Google Gemini API: processes requests and generates replies.
  • Telegram: delivers messages between Jarvis and the owner.
  • Microsoft speech service: turns reply text into audio when a voice reply is used.

Data is not shared with anyone else, unless the owner asks Jarvis to send it, for example in an email.

Storage

Access tokens, conversation history and related records are stored on a private server controlled by the owner. Calendar events and emails stay in Google's services and are fetched when needed.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account third-party connections.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on this site's repository.
